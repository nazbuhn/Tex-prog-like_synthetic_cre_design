Borzoi-only V4 design experiment

Fine-tuned 2048-bp Borzoi checkpoint from matched_v1 is frozen. Native CREsted
in_silico_evolution (ISE) exhaustively evaluates all three alternative DNA
bases at every one of 1000 sequence positions, for 200 edit iterations.
Predictions are RC-averaged and normalized to FRPM for the ATAC-defined C1
target and the ten other target-panel output classes. Centered 1000-bp
replacement DNA is inserted into immutable 2048-bp contexts; the original
Borzoi model weights, training data, and Python environment are unchanged.

Library: 256 designs (128 natural starts from at most 50 vetted parent peaks;
128 composition-matched random starts), each with 25% or 50% target-floor
retention assignment balanced by arm. Nature of context: 2 optimization,
4 selection, 8 audit windows drawn from training DNA and excluded from pilot.
Per-candidate objective = robust mean/worst log1p(C1) - log1p(strongest
competitor) minus 10 times the mean C1 floor shortfall. Entire 201-sequence
trajectory retained; the best step satisfying optimization-context floors is
selected post hoc; otherwise minimum-penalty solution selected and explicitly
marked nonfeasible. Final-audit contexts not used in selection.

The pipeline applies no known PWM or motif information. 16 dinucleotide
shuffles of selected sequences control for composition in selection and audit.
Scores are predictions, not cell-state-exclusive enhancer activity; training
DNA contexts do not constitute a true held-out sequence validation.
No secondary CREsted predictor is loaded, and no model is trained or fine-tuned.
